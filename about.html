About Me

I am a first-year student who loves travelling around our island. I built Lanka Explorer on my own because planning a trip across Sri Lanka usually means jumping between five different websites for places, prices and weather. This site brings all of that into one simple, friendly place. Every destination here is researched by me, with entry fees and travel seasons checked against official sources.

This is an individual mini project, so I plan the work, write the content, build the data file and design the pages myself, week by week.
